08 — FAQ

প্রায়শই জিজ্ঞাসিত প্রশ্ন

How do I apply for admission?

Visit the school office during the admission window shown under "Admission open" above, or call the number listed in the contact section for the current form and seat availability.

How do parents access the ERP portal?

Each guardian receives sign-in details from the class teacher once admission is confirmed. Use the "ERP Login" button at the top of this page to sign in.

What are the school hours?

The school office is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, as noted in the footer. Class hours are shared separately with each section.

I forgot my ERP password — what do I do?

Contact the school office with your child's roll and class, and they'll reset it for you — the same note appears next to the ERP login card below.
